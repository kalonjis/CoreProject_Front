How to use backup codes:

  1. Go to the login page and enter your username and password
  2. When prompted for 2FA, look for "Use backup code" or "Can't access your device?"
  3. Enter one of your backup codes exactly as provided
  4. The code will be consumed and cannot be used again
  5. Consider regenerating new codes when you have 3 or fewer remaining
